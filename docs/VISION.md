# Vision

Covers look and feel only. The domain (what is simulated) is still a team decision and is not recorded here.

## Art direction (Justin, 2026-09-22)

Reference games:
- West of Loathing
- Don't Starve Together
- Deadly Days: Roadtrip
- Octopath Traveler 0
- Paper Mario

Common thread: flat, hand-drawn or pixel characters and props set in a world with depth. Most of the references put 2D sprites inside a 3D scene with a perspective or tilted camera, 3D lighting, shadows, and depth of field (Octopath's "HD-2D", Paper Mario, Don't Starve). West of Loathing and Deadly Days are closer to flat 2D.
